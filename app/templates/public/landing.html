Core System Architecture

Designed for rigor, transparency, and intent.

01

Application Lifecycle Tracking

Manage applications across structured states: Saved, Applied, Assessment, Interview, Offer, Rejected, and Withdrawn. Keep every transition consistent with atomic transactional status history logs.

02

Interview Round Management

Track multi-round hiring loops per job with meeting links, interviewer feedback, round numbers, preparation notes, and upcoming timelines visible directly on your dashboard.

03

Resume Reference Registry

Maintain versioned links to external resume documents and associate specific resume revisions with each application without unnecessary server-side document storage bloat.

04

In-App Reminders & Deadlines

Never miss an assessment deadline or follow-up email. Set time-bound in-app reminders linked to specific job records with database-enforced deduplication alerts.

05

AI-Assisted Resume Matching

Harness Google Gemini to compare your resume text against targeted job descriptions. Receive structured feedback with a transparent four-part Compatibility Score (Skills, Experience, Education, and Keywords) without misleading probability claims.
